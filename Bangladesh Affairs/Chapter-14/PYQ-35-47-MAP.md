## Priority model questions (not verbatim PYQs)

1. “Globalization is an opportunity as well as a challenge for Bangladesh.” Discuss.
2. Assess the contribution of RMG, remittances and FDI to global integration.
3. Compare the roles of WTO, IMF, World Bank, ADB and IsDB.
4. How can Bangladesh diversify exports and increase domestic value addition?
5. Evaluate the implications of LDC graduation for trade preferences and competitiveness.
6. Discuss globalization, labour rights, inequality and decent work.
7. How can economic diplomacy improve Bangladesh’s global position?
8. Analyse global price shocks and economic resilience.
9. Discuss globalization, climate action and green industrialization.
10. Develop a policy framework for inclusive and sustainable globalization.
