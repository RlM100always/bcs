## Cross-year pattern: what the evidence actually supports

1. **Direct political-party themes:** 35th BCS (party system, party functions, opposition politics).
2. **Direct political-culture theme:** 47th BCS (20-mark analytical question on political culture and democratic deviations).
3. **Related political-process themes:** 36th (RPO, election observers, press freedom, constitutional amendments), 40th (political leadership), 46th (elections and Election Commission), 47th (speech/press freedom).
4. **Unverified years are not “zero-question” years:** 37th, 38th, 41st, 43rd, 44th and some adjacent years require full-paper item-by-item verification.
5. **Special examinations:** 39th and 42nd should not be forced into a standard general Bangladesh Affairs written-paper comparison.
6. **Do not claim frequency from incomplete evidence.** “Important” below means syllabus relevance, conceptual centrality and known archive evidence—not a statistical forecast or guarantee of appearing.

## Constitutional and legal references to verify before the exam

- **Constitution Article 11:** democracy and effective participation through elected representatives.
- **Article 38:** freedom of association, subject to constitutional/legal limits.
- **Article 39:** thought, conscience and speech freedoms, subject to the text’s limitations.
- **Article 70:** party-linked consequences for a member elected on a party nomination voting against the party / resignation as specified by the article.
- **Representation of the People Order, 1972, Articles 90A–90H:** party registration, eligibility, disqualification, application, registration and cancellation provisions. Always consult the current amended text.
- **Bangladesh Election Commission:** current registered-party list; status can change and must be checked on the official site.
