## Evidence-based preparation priorities (model questions, not guaranteed predictions)

1. Constitutional independence and duties of the Election Commission (Articles 118–120).
2. Voter rolls, adult franchise and equal voting rights (Articles 121–122).
3. Election timing, legal framework, election petitions and executive assistance (Articles 123–126).
4. End-to-end election management: schedule, nominations, scrutiny, campaign, polling, counting and results.
5. Conditions for free, fair, credible and inclusive elections.
6. Election observation versus election administration.
7. Campaign finance disclosure, code of conduct and lawful enforcement.
8. Technology, cybersecurity, privacy, AI-generated misinformation and transparent audit.
9. Women, disabled voters, minorities, first-time voters and access barriers.
10. Current legal amendments: RPO Amendment Act 2026, rules and EC circulars.
