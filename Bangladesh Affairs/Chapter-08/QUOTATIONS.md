# Chapter 08 — English Quotations with Sources
## Legislature · Executive · Judiciary · Rule of Law

> পরীক্ষায় ১টি প্রাসঙ্গিক quotation ব্যবহার করে নিজের ভাষায় বাংলাদেশের সাংবিধানিক প্রেক্ষাপটে ব্যাখ্যা করুন। মূল উৎসে wording মিলিয়ে নিন।

1. **“All human beings are born free and equal in dignity and rights.”**  
   **Source:** Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR), Article 1. **Use:** equality before law, dignity, rights.  
   https://www.un.org/en/about-us/universal-declaration-of-human-rights

2. **“Everyone is entitled in full equality to a fair and public hearing by an independent and impartial tribunal”**  
   **Source:** UDHR, Article 10 (excerpt). **Use:** judicial independence, fair trial.  
   https://www.un.org/en/about-us/universal-declaration-of-human-rights

3. **“The will of the people shall be the basis of the authority of government.”**  
   **Source:** UDHR, Article 21(3). **Use:** representative government and accountability.  
   https://www.un.org/en/about-us/universal-declaration-of-human-rights

4. **“No one shall be subjected to arbitrary arrest, detention or exile.”**  
   **Source:** UDHR, Article 9. **Use:** due process and executive power.  
   https://www.un.org/en/about-us/universal-declaration-of-human-rights

5. **“Everyone has the right to an effective remedy”**  
   **Source:** UDHR, Article 8 (excerpt). **Use:** constitutional remedies and access to justice.  
   https://www.un.org/en/about-us/universal-declaration-of-human-rights

6. **“The Constitution is the supreme law of the Republic”**  
   **Source:** Constitution of Bangladesh, Article 7(2), excerpt. **Use:** constitutional supremacy.  
   https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-print-367.html

7. **“The High Court Division shall have superintendence and control over all courts and tribunals subordinate to it.”**  
   **Source:** Constitution of Bangladesh, Article 109. **Use:** judicial structure.  
   https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-chapter-print-688.html

8. **“Power tends to corrupt, and absolute power corrupts absolutely.”**  
   **Source:** Lord Acton, letter to Bishop Mandell Creighton (1887), familiar excerpt. **Use:** checks and balances. The quotation is a short excerpt; consult the original letter for context.  
   https://www.acton.org/

9. **“The law is reason, free from passion.”**  
   **Source:** Aristotle, *Politics*, Book III, 1287a; translation varies. **Use:** impartial adjudication.  
   https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/

10. **“We the people”**  
    **Source:** Preamble to the Constitution of the United States (1787). **Use:** popular sovereignty in comparative constitutional discussion, not as Bangladeshi constitutional text.  
    https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/constitution-transcript

11. **“Justice delayed is justice denied.”**  
    **Source:** established legal maxim; no single author claimed. **Use:** case backlog and timely justice.

12. **“The powers of government should be divided among different departments.”**  
    **Status:** paraphrase of separation-of-powers doctrine, not a direct quotation. **Use:** explain the principle without quotation marks.

13. **“That government is best which governs least.”**  
    **Source note:** popularly associated with Henry David Thoreau; context and attribution are often simplified. Use cautiously, not as a universal rule for public administration.  
    https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/71

14. **“The price of liberty is eternal vigilance.”**  
    **Source note:** attribution to Thomas Jefferson is disputed. Do not confidently attribute it to Jefferson; omit if strict sourcing is required.

15. **“Equal justice under law.”**  
    **Source:** inscription on the United States Supreme Court building; comparative motto only, not Bangladesh constitutional text.  
    https://www.supremecourt.gov/

16. **“The rule of law is the foundation of a democratic society.”**  
    **Status:** general synthesis, not a verified direct quotation from a named source. Use as your own sentence, without quotation marks.

## Original exam-ready lines (not quotations)
Use these without attribution or quotation marks:
- Independence protects institutions from improper pressure; accountability protects the public from arbitrary power.
- A strong Parliament is measured not only by laws passed, but by the quality of scrutiny and public outcomes.
- Judicial independence has practical value only when people can access, afford and enforce remedies.
- The rule of law requires fair procedures, equal protection and effective remedies—not merely the existence of statutes.
- Institutional balance works best when every organ respects constitutional limits and fulfils its responsibilities.
