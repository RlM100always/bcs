## Answer strategy by command word
- **Explain:** define the concept, cite the relevant article, explain significance.
- **Analyse:** show institutional roles, causes, effects and interactions.
- **Critically evaluate:** state the rationale, limitations, counterarguments and balanced conclusion.
- **Suggest reforms:** specify institution, action, safeguard and measurable result.
- **Rights/rule of law:** connect constitutional text to access, remedy, enforcement and lived experience.

## Priority articles to revise
- Article 7: supremacy of the Constitution.
- Article 22: separation of the judiciary from the executive as a state policy.
- Article 55: Cabinet and collective responsibility.
- Article 65: Parliament and legislative power.
- Article 70: party resignation/voting and vacation of seat.
- Article 76: parliamentary standing committees.
- Article 94: Supreme Court and judicial independence.
- Article 102: specified writ jurisdiction of the High Court Division.
- Article 109: superintendence/control of subordinate courts and tribunals.
- Article 111: binding effect of Supreme Court declarations of law.
- Article 112: authorities to act in aid of the Supreme Court.
