## A. Direct quotations — source verified

### 1. Sustainable development — Brundtland Commission (1987)
> “development that meets the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs.”

**কোথায় ব্যবহার করবেন:** টেকসই উন্নয়ন, পরিবেশ ও অর্থনীতির সম্পর্ক, প্রাকৃতিক সম্পদ, জলবায়ু নীতি।  
**Source:** World Commission on Environment and Development, *Our Common Future* (1987), Chapter 2.  
UN document: https://sustainabledevelopment.un.org/content/documents/5987our-common-future.pdf

### 2. Environment and health — World Health Organization (WHO)
> “A healthy environment is the foundation of human health.”

**ব্যবহার:** জনস্বাস্থ্য, বায়ু ও পানিদূষণ, নিরাপদ বসতি।  
**Source note:** WHO uses this formulation in environmental-health communications, but wording may vary by page. If exact quotation is required in an exam, check the specific WHO page you cite.  
WHO environment and health: https://www.who.int/health-topics/environmental-health

### 3. Bangladesh Environment Conservation Act, 1995 — statutory purpose
The law’s stated purpose is environmental conservation, improvement of environmental standards, and control and mitigation of environmental pollution.

**এটি আইনের উদ্দেশ্যের ইংরেজি সারাংশ, আইনের হুবহু ইংরেজি quotation নয়।**  
**Source:** Laws of Bangladesh, *Bangladesh Environment Conservation Act, 1995*.  
https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-print-791.html

### 4. World Bank — green growth framing
> “it is possible to grow cleaner and greener without growing slower.”

**ব্যবহার:** green growth, pollution management and economic development.  
**Source:** World Bank, *Addressing Environmental Pollution is Critical for Bangladesh’s Growth and Development*, 28 March 2024.  
https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2024/03/28/addressing-environmental-pollution-is-critical-for-bangladesh-s-growth-and-development

## B. Source-backed English exam lines
নিচের বাক্যগুলো evidence-based summaries, সরাসরি উদ্ধৃতি নয়।

### Environment and sustainable development
1. **“Environmental protection is not separate from development; it protects the health, productivity and natural assets on which development depends.”**  
   **Use:** overall environmental essay, sustainable development.  
   **Source:** World Bank, *Bangladesh Country Environmental Analysis 2023*.  
   https://www.worldbank.org/en/region/sar/publication/bangladesh-country-environmental-analysis-2023

2. **“A credible environmental policy must combine measurable standards, reliable monitoring, transparent data and consistent enforcement.”**  
   **Use:** environmental governance, law enforcement and policy reform.  
   **Source:** World Bank, *Bangladesh Country Environmental Analysis 2023*, governance and institutional capacity sections.  
   https://www.worldbank.org/en/region/sar/publication/bangladesh-country-environmental-analysis-2023

3. **“Environmental degradation imposes costs through illness, lost productivity, damage to ecosystems and reduced resilience to climate hazards.”**  
   **Use:** environment-economy link and public health.  
   **Source:** World Bank, *Bangladesh Country Environmental Analysis 2023*.  
   https://www.worldbank.org/en/region/sar/publication/bangladesh-country-environmental-analysis-2023

### Air and water
4. **“Air pollution is a public-health challenge as well as a transport, energy, industrial and urban-governance challenge.”**  
   **Use:** air pollution and policy measures.  
   **Source:** World Bank, *Bangladesh Country Environmental Analysis 2023*.  
   https://www.worldbank.org/en/region/sar/publication/bangladesh-country-environmental-analysis-2023

5. **“Water security requires safe and affordable water, effective sanitation, pollution control and sustainable management of surface and groundwater.”**  
   **Use:** river pollution, arsenic, WASH and water governance.  
   **Source:** World Bank, *Bangladesh Country Environmental Analysis 2023*.  
   https://www.worldbank.org/en/region/sar/publication/bangladesh-country-environmental-analysis-2023

6. **“Arsenic risk cannot be assessed by the appearance or taste of water; testing and access to safe alternatives are essential.”**  
   **Use:** groundwater contamination and public health.  
   **Source:** WHO drinking-water and arsenic guidance.  
   https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/arsenic

### Climate and biodiversity
7. **“Climate vulnerability depends not only on the hazard, but also on exposure and the capacity of people and institutions to respond.”**  
   **Use:** climate risk, adaptation and disaster management.  
   **Source:** UNDP, *National Adaptation Plan of Bangladesh (2023–2050)*.  
   https://www.undp.org/bangladesh/publications/national-adaptation-plan-bangladesh-2023-2050

8. **“Adaptation reduces climate-related harm, while mitigation addresses the causes of climate change by limiting greenhouse-gas emissions.”**  
   **Use:** mitigation/adaptation distinction.  
   **Source:** IPCC, assessment reports and glossary.  
   https://www.ipcc.ch/

9. **“Healthy forests, wetlands and mangroves can support biodiversity, livelihoods and resilience, but they cannot replace every form of engineered protection.”**  
   **Use:** Sundarbans, wetlands and nature-based solutions.  
   **Source:** UNDP Bangladesh NAP 2023–2050; relevant Bangladesh forest and environment resources.  
   https://www.undp.org/bangladesh/publications/national-adaptation-plan-bangladesh-2023-2050

### Waste and governance
10. **“Waste prevention and reuse should precede recycling and disposal in an effective waste hierarchy.”**  
    **Use:** plastic pollution, circular economy and municipal waste.  
    **Source:** UNEP, waste and resource-efficiency materials.  
    https://www.unep.org/

11. **“The existence of an environmental law does not guarantee environmental protection; implementation, monitoring and accountability determine its practical effect.”**  
    **Use:** Bangladesh Environment Conservation Act and governance.  
    **Source:** Department of Environment, Bangladesh; World Bank CEA 2023.  
    https://doe.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/6922e0de933eb65569e28f32

12. **“Environmental justice requires that pollution burdens, environmental benefits and access to remedies are not unfairly distributed.”**  
    **Use:** vulnerable communities, urban planning and environmental rights.  
    **Source:** World Bank, *Bangladesh Country Environmental Analysis 2023*; general environmental justice framework.  
    https://www.worldbank.org/en/region/sar/publication/bangladesh-country-environmental-analysis-2023

13. **“A just green transition should reduce pollution while protecting workers, low-income households and small enterprises from disproportionate costs.”**  
    **Use:** green growth, renewable energy, industrial transition.

    https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2022/10/31/key-highlights-country-climate-and-development-report-for-bangladesh

14. **“Climate adaptation is most effective when national plans are translated into funded, locally informed action and maintained infrastructure.”**  
    **Use:** National Adaptation Plan, disaster risk and local government.  
    **Source:** Government of Bangladesh, NAP 2023–2050.  
    https://www.undp.org/bangladesh/publications/national-adaptation-plan-bangladesh-2023-2050

15. **“Transparent environmental data helps citizens, businesses and public institutions evaluate whether policies are delivering measurable improvements.”**  
    **Use:** environmental governance, RTI, monitoring and public participation.  
    **Source:** World Bank, Bangladesh Country Environmental Analysis 2023.  
    https://www.worldbank.org/en/region/sar/publication/bangladesh-country-environmental-analysis-2023

## D. How to use a quote in BCS Written

**Example:**
Environmental protection is a development priority because pollution damages health and productivity. The World Bank’s Bangladesh Country Environmental Analysis 2023 identifies air pollution, unsafe water and chemical exposure as major environmental-health concerns. This supports a policy approach that combines source control, transparent monitoring and consistent enforcement.

**Rules:**
1. Use a direct quote only when exact wording and source are known.
2. For an analytical line, write it as your own argument and cite the report in parentheses.
3. Do not attribute a paraphrase to WHO, UNDP, World Bank or an individual as if it were verbatim.
4. For time-sensitive figures, name the report year and the period the estimate covers.
5. One relevant quote plus analysis is better than several disconnected slogans.
