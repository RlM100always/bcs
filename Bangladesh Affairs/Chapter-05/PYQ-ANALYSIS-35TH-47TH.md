## 4. Priority ranking from verified themes

### Priority A — must prepare first
1. Environmental degradation: definition, causes, consequences and remedies.
2. River pollution and integrated water management.
3. Waste hierarchy, plastic pollution and circular economy.
4. Climate change, sea-level rise and adaptation in Bangladesh.
5. Disaster-management cycle and agriculture/livelihood resilience.
6. Forest resources, biodiversity and the Sundarbans.
7. Sustainable development and green growth.
8. Ecologically Critical Areas (ECA): legal meaning, significance, examples and management.

### Priority B — prepare next
- Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) and environmental clearance.
- Air pollution, PM2.5 and public health.
- Wetlands, river encroachment and urban drainage.
- Climate-related displacement and environmental justice.
- Environmental law, institutional coordination and the implementation gap.
- Nature-based solutions and protected-area governance.
- Marine-resource use, coastal ecology and blue economy.

## 5. Reusable 20-mark answer architecture

1. **Introduction:** define the issue and explain its relevance to Bangladesh.
2. **Evidence:** name one official law, national plan or institutional report; date every statistic.
3. **Causes:** structural, economic, behavioural and governance-related factors.
4. **Consequences:** health, livelihoods, economy, ecosystem and distributional impacts.
5. **Policy assessment:** laws, institutions, achievements and implementation gaps.
6. **Recommendations:** immediate, medium-term and long-term actions.
7. **Accountability:** identify the responsible institution and measurable indicators.
8. **Conclusion:** connect environmental protection with public health, justice and sustainable development.
