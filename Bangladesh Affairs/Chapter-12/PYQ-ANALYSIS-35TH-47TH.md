## 3. What the archive suggests about exam preparation

The directly relevant prompts point to a recurring syllabus cluster rather than a statistically validated frequency ranking:

1. **E-governance and public-service delivery** — 35th and 45th archive-reported prompts.
2. **ICT progress and digitalisation** — 37th and 38th.
3. **Digital Bangladesh and technology's social impact** — 41st and 47th.
4. **Media freedom and accountability** — 36th.
5. **Digital procurement** — 36th e-tendering prompt.
6. **Technology in education** — 47th.
7. **RTI and cybersecurity/privacy** — essential official-syllabus areas even though no direct prompt was authenticated in the years reviewed.

This does **not** mean that an item is statistically more likely to appear in the next examination. It means these are sensible coverage priorities because they are explicit in the BPSC syllabus and are represented in the archive-reported questions.

## 6. Research limitations

- The public archive is a secondary source and warns that it is not a collection of authenticated original BPSC scans.
- Exact wording, question numbering and marks must be checked against the original paper before claiming “verified PYQ”.
- 39th and 42nd do not provide a comparable regular general BCS Written Bangladesh Affairs paper in the archive index used here.
- The archive says the 46th set is incomplete.
- This is a chapter-specific thematic audit, not a claim that every question in all BCS papers has been reproduced.
- “Priority” means a sensible syllabus-based preparation order, not a forecast or a statistically proven probability.
