> পরীক্ষায় ১–২টি প্রাসঙ্গিক উদ্ধৃতি যথেষ্ট। উৎস ও প্রেক্ষাপট উল্লেখ করুন; paraphrase-কে quotation হিসেবে লিখবেন না।

## Development and human capability

1. **“Development can be seen ... as a process of expanding the real freedoms that people enjoy.”**\
   **Source:** Amartya Sen, *Development as Freedom* (1999), Introduction; ellipsis indicates omitted text. Use for human development, capabilities and inclusive growth. [Oxford University Press](https://global.oup.com/academic/product/development-as-freedom-9780192893307)

2. **“The basic purpose of development is to enlarge people's choices.”**\
   **Source:** UNDP, *Human Development Report 1990*; verify punctuation in the original report. Use for education, health and quality of life. [Primary report](https://hdr.undp.org/content/human-development-report-1990)

3. **“Leave no one behind.”**\
   **Source:** UN, *Transforming our world: the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development* (2015). Use for poverty reduction, inclusion and social protection. [UN source](https://sdgs.un.org/2030agenda)

4. **“End poverty in all its forms everywhere.”**\
   **Source:** UN Sustainable Development Goal 1 title. Use for poverty and social policy. [UN source](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal1)

5. **“Ensure inclusive and equitable quality education and promote lifelong learning opportunities for all.”**\
   **Source:** UN Sustainable Development Goal 4 title. Use for education, skills and human capital. [UN source](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal4)

6. **“Promote sustained, inclusive and sustainable economic growth, full and productive employment and decent work for all.”**\
   **Source:** UN Sustainable Development Goal 8 title. Use for employment, RMG and economic transformation. [UN source](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal8)

7. **“Build resilient infrastructure, promote inclusive and sustainable industrialization and foster innovation.”**\
   **Source:** UN Sustainable Development Goal 9 title. Use for industrial policy, productivity and export diversification. [UN source](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal9)

8. **“Ensure sustainable consumption and production patterns.”**\
   **Source:** UN Sustainable Development Goal 12 title. Use for green industry, textile waste and responsible production. [UN source](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal12)

## Literature, language and culture

9. **“Where the mind is without fear and the head is held high”**\
   **Source:** Rabindranath Tagore, “Where the Mind is Without Fear,” *Gitanjali*, poem 35 in common English editions. Use for education, dignity and freedom of thought. [Nobel Prize — Tagore](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/literature/1913/tagore/biographical/)

10. **“The highest education is that which does not merely give us information but makes our life in harmony with all existence.”**\
    **Source:** Rabindranath Tagore, *Personality* (1917), essay “My School”; verify exact wording in the edition used. Use for holistic education and culture. [Internet Archive catalogue](https://archive.org/)

11. **“The will of the people shall be the basis of the authority of government.”**\
    **Source:** Universal Declaration of Human Rights, Article 21(3). Use for cultural participation, citizenship and inclusive public life. [UN UDHR](https://www.un.org/en/about-us/universal-declaration-of-human-rights)

12. **“All human beings are born free and equal in dignity and rights.”**\
    **Source:** Universal Declaration of Human Rights, Article 1. Use for equal access to services, dignity and inclusion. [UN UDHR](https://www.un.org/en/about-us/universal-declaration-of-human-rights)

## Work, equality and sustainability

13. **“Equal pay for work of equal value”**\
    **Source:** ILO, *Equal Remuneration Convention, 1951 (No. 100)*, Article 1; treaty phrase. Use for gender equality and labour rights. [ILO](https://www.ilo.org/)

14. **“Promote full and productive employment and decent work for all”**\
    **Source:** UN SDG target 8.5, excerpt. Use for youth employment, worker protection and productivity. [UN SDG 8](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal8)

15. **“Achieve gender equality and empower all women and girls.”**\
    **Source:** UN Sustainable Development Goal 5 title. Use for women's employment and inclusive development. [UN SDG 5](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal5)

16. **“Take urgent action to combat climate change and its impacts.”**\
    **Source:** UN Sustainable Development Goal 13 title. Use for climate-resilient agriculture, industry and urbanisation. [UN SDG 13](https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal13)

## Original exam-ready lines (not direct quotations)

- Growth is a means; the test of development is whether people gain capabilities, security and meaningful choices.
- A resilient economy diversifies production, develops human capital and protects people against shocks.
- Cultural identity is strongest when shared history coexists with pluralism and creative freedom.
- Export success becomes sustainable when productivity, worker safety, environmental performance and value addition improve together.
- Development statistics are useful only when definitions, reference periods and limitations are clear.
- Public policy should evaluate not only how much is spent, but who benefits and whether outcomes improve.
