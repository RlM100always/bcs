## 3. Cross-paper patterns

1. **“Discuss / analyse”** — define the issue, explain causes, identify consequences, then evaluate policies.
2. **“Achievements and challenges”** — balance progress with distribution, implementation and sustainability.
3. **“Compare the 1970s and today”** — use a table: employment, urbanisation, education, language/media, technology, gender and culture. Avoid invented figures.
4. **“Recommend priority sectors”** — justify each sector by productivity, jobs, export potential, inclusion, climate resilience and feasibility.
5. **“Poverty alleviation”** — combine productive employment, services, social protection, skills and regional inclusion.
6. **“Textile industry”** — historical context + current competitiveness + worker welfare + environmental performance + value addition.
7. **“Culture and society”** — connect literature/media/identity with pluralism, intergenerational change and cultural preservation.
