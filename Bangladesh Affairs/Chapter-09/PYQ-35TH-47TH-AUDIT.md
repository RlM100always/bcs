## 1. Directly verified archive evidence

### 35th BCS Written — International Affairs, not Bangladesh Affairs
The public transcript of the 35th BCS **International Affairs (Subject Code 007)** includes:
- short note on internal determinants of foreign policy;
- Bangladesh–India bilateral relations and connectivity;
- UN Security Council composition, responsibilities and reform;
- a case-based diplomatic response to suspension of US GSP benefits.

These are highly relevant foreign-policy practice themes, **but they belong to International Affairs, not Bangladesh Affairs**. Do not mislabel them as a Bangladesh Affairs paper. Sources: [35th BCS archive](https://bcsexam.org/written-questions/35th-bcs-written) and [Live Written transcript](https://web.livewritten.com/35th-bcs-written-question-pdf/).

### 35th BCS Written — Bangladesh Affairs
The archive also includes a 20-mark essay prompt asking candidates to define Bangladesh’s foreign policy and discuss its principles, objectives, strengths and limitations. This is a direct Bangladesh Affairs theme and should be treated as a high-priority anchor. Secondary-source warning still applies: verify against the original BPSC question paper where available. [35th BCS archive](https://bcsexam.org/written-questions/35th-bcs-written).

## 5. Exam use
For a 20-mark answer, cite Article 25 where relevant, use one sourced quotation at most, explain both opportunities and constraints, and end with 4–6 actionable recommendations. Never invent PYQ wording, marks or year-frequency claims.
