## What the evidence supports

1. **Directly evidenced high-priority themes:** the 1947–1971 background; the Language Movement; Six-Point Programme; 1969 mass uprising; 1970 election; 7 March speech; Mujibnagar Government; war crimes; and the “spirit of Liberation War” in national development.
2. **Recurring question formats:** short notes on a date/event; causal analysis of several historical events; chronological account from Language Movement to Liberation War; essay on the Liberation War's values; book review or dialogue using historical memory.
3. **Answer-writing implication:** build one integrated chronology, then prepare modular 5/10/20-mark answers for each turning point. Do not assume a theme will repeat merely because it appeared in one paper.
